# One-pager

This is the exact format we ask for at the end of the working session: one page, five sections, written for a decision. Keep it to a page. The section that most often separates strong candidates is the last one — what you deliberately left out.

Replace everything in italics.

---

**Recommendation:** _One or two sentences. What should we do first, and why. Lead with the decision, not the background._

## Problem

_What problem are you actually solving, and for whom? Name the user and what is going wrong for them. One short paragraph._

## Evidence

_What in the data supports this, and how confident are you? Cite the specific numbers. Say which numbers you did not trust and why — this is where you show your data judgment, not hide it._

## What you'd ship

_The concrete thing you would build or change first. Scope it to what is worth doing now, not everything that could be done._

## What you'd measure

_The one or two metrics that would tell you this worked. Non-vanity: a number that moves only if the problem actually got better._

## What you deliberately left out

_What you chose not to do, and why. Real cuts with reasons. "Nothing" is the wrong answer; so is a list of everything you didn't have time for._
