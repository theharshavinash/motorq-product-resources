# The interview process

One track, applied consistently to everyone, from campus APM interns to experienced PMs. The bar we hold you to reflects your experience; the process does not change. There is no coding or DSA test anywhere in this loop — that is not what we are hiring for on the product track, and preparing for one would be wasted effort.

Two things run through the whole loop, so we state them once here. AI tools are allowed and expected — most visibly in the working session, but you may use them anywhere they help. And we expect no prior knowledge of telematics, automotive, or connected-vehicle data. We supply the domain context in the room. All engineering, design, and other backgrounds are welcome; a non-CS degree is not a disadvantage.

---

## 0. Application form

**Async · 10–20 minutes · reviewed by one grader.**

Three graded written answers. We are reading for clarity of thought and evidence that you have actually built or shipped something — a named user, a mechanism, a metric, an honest tradeoff. This is a real gate, not a formality; most of a strong application lives or dies here.

## 1. Product sense

**Live · 60 minutes · one PM.**

A case built around an unfamiliar B2B user — typically a fleet operations manager. We are watching how you frame an ambiguous problem, whether you can build empathy for a user whose job you have never done, and how you prioritise once the problem is clear. There is no single right answer; there are well-reasoned and poorly-reasoned ones.

## 2. Working session

**Live · about 2.5 hours · one PM plus one data/engineering interviewer.**

The centre of the loop, in three parts:

- **Data exploration (60 min).** A messy synthetic dataset and an open question. Find what matters, distrust your numbers, size a recommendation.
- **AI-assisted prototype (60 min).** Build a demoable version of what you would ship. AI tools are expected here. The prototype itself proves little — what we score is the judgment you narrate while building it: why this scope, what you verified, what you deliberately left out.
- **Demo and one-pager (15 min).** Show it, and hand over a one-page written summary.

A polished prototype built in silence does not pass this round. Think out loud.

You can practise the data-exploration and one-pager parts end to end against the dataset in [`../practice/`](../practice/).

## 3. Technical collaboration

**Live · 60 minutes · one senior engineer.**

A working conversation, not a quiz. We are looking for technical fluency and feasibility instincts: can you reason about tradeoffs — freshness against cost, push against pull, what "real-time" commits us to — and spec something an engineer could actually build. You will not write code.

## 4. Ownership and leadership

**Behavioural · 45 minutes · Head of Product.**

Past-tense only. Real decisions you made without enough information, times you were wrong, the hardest no you delivered, things you built or ran without being asked. We are assessing drive, judgment, and whether how you work matches how we work.

---

**On CGPA:** it is a coarse floor, not a ranking key. We do not rank candidates by CGPA, and a strong application clears the floor comfortably regardless of where exactly it sits.
