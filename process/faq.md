# FAQ

**Is there a coding or DSA test?**
No. There is no coding, DSA, or whiteboard-algorithm test anywhere on the product track. The technical round is a conversation about tradeoffs and feasibility, and you will not write code in it. Preparing DSA for this role is wasted effort.

**Are AI tools allowed?**
Yes — allowed and expected, most visibly in the working session where you build a prototype. We are not testing whether you can avoid AI; we are testing the judgment you show while using it. Use whatever tool you are fastest in.

**Do I need to know telematics, automotive, or connected-vehicle data?**
No. We expect zero prior domain knowledge and supply the context you need in the room. Nobody is filtered out for not knowing the industry.

**Do I need a CS degree? What about other branches?**
Any branch is welcome. We hire mechanical, electrical, civil, and non-engineering backgrounds onto the product track. A non-CS degree is not a disadvantage. If you are moving from engineering into product, see [`../prep/engineers-to-pm.md`](../prep/engineers-to-pm.md).

**How much does CGPA matter?**
It is a coarse floor, not a ranking key. We do not order candidates by CGPA, and a strong application clears the floor comfortably. Do not let a middling CGPA stop you from applying.

**What is the difference between the fresher and experienced tracks?**
The process is the same for everyone. The bar we hold you to reflects your experience — we expect more depth and more owned outcomes from an experienced PM than from a campus intern — but the stages, the format, and what we score do not change.

**What happens after the application form closes?**
Graders review the written answers. Shortlisted candidates are contacted to schedule the live loop (stages 1–4). If you are not shortlisted, we will tell you; we do not leave applications open-ended.

**Can I reapply if I am not selected?**
Yes. Reapply in a future cycle. We do not hold a past outcome against a new application — a year of building changes a lot.

**How should I prepare?**
Start with [`../README.md`](../README.md) and [`../prep/`](../prep/). There is a full practice dataset and brief in [`../practice/`](../practice/). We deliberately do not publish a study plan — how you prepare is part of what we are assessing.
