# Three playlists

Depth beats breadth, and there is more PM content on YouTube than anyone could watch usefully. These three cover the whole surface between them. If you watch nothing else, watch a few full mocks from the first one — it is the closest preview of what our live rounds sound like.

**Exponent — Product Management Interviews.** Real mock interviews across product design, analytics, strategy, execution, and technical rounds. The most direct preview of what our live rounds feel like from the other side of the table.
https://www.youtube.com/@tryexponent

**Diego Granados.** Answer structure and frameworks, pitched at people entering PM rather than at senior PMs, with recent coverage of AI PM roles. Use it for the shape of a good answer.
https://www.youtube.com/@PMDiegoGranados

**Lenny's Podcast.** Not interview prep, and do not treat it as such. This is where product judgment comes from — the thing our rubric rewards and the thing frameworks cannot fake. Listen to it over months, not the week before.
https://www.youtube.com/@LennysPodcast
